Branch office network

A single WAN uplink lands on the edge firewall, which hands off to a core switch trunking two VLANs — a wired data segment and a Wi-Fi segment serving 42 aggregated clients behind one access point.

THE HEADLINE

One firewall, one WAN circuit

Every packet in or out of the branch crosses `edge-fw-01` over a single WAN circuit. There's no second uplink and no HA pair — a firewall or ISP outage takes the whole site offline.

Two VLANs off one trunk

  • `core-sw-01` trunks VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 to their zones
  • VLAN 10 carries the wired file server only
  • VLAN 20 carries all wireless traffic

42 clients, one access point

`ap-floor2` is drawn once with a client-count badge rather than 42 individual endpoint nodes — the segment's existence is the story, not each device on it.
